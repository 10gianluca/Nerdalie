Schotten Totten

Nine border stones, two clans, online with a friend

or join a friend's game
or play on your own against the computer
How to play
  • There are nine stones between you. Each stone has three card slots on your side and three on the other player's.
  • On your turn play one card to your side of any stone that isn't won yet, then draw one. You hold six cards.
  • When a side has three cards it makes a formation. Best to worst: colour run (same colour, in order), three of a kind, colour (same colour), run (in order, any colours), then sum (anything else). The same formation is settled by the higher total, and if that is level too, by whoever finished first.
  • You win a stone when your finished side can't be beaten any more, even by cards nobody has played yet. That is checked for you every turn.
  • Win the game with five stones, or three stones side by side.
