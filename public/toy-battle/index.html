How to play
  • Two armies of toy troops fight over a board of bases joined by paths. You win by placing a troop on the enemy H.Q., or by being first to win the number of medals shown for your side.
  • On your turn do one thing: draw 2 troops from your reserve onto your rack (it holds 8), or place 1 troop on the board and use its effect.
  • A troop can go on an empty base, on one of your own, or on an enemy base holding a strictly weaker troop (a stack builds up, but only the top troop counts). Every placement must be connected to your H.Q. by paths running only through bases you hold.
  • Hold every base around a region and you take its medals, and keep them even if you lose a base later.
  • If a player can't draw or place a troop, the game ends: more medals wins, and on a tie the player who got stuck loses.
  • Each troop has its own effect (tap Troops & terrain in a game to read them), and each battlefield has special bases.

The troops, their strengths and effects, the special bases and the rules follow the official game. The eight battlefield layouts here are this app's own designs, and the artwork is original.
