Mancala

Sow your seeds, steal your opponents, online with a friend

or join a friend's game
or play on your own against the computer
How to play
  • Each player has six pits and a store on the right. Every pit starts with four seeds.
  • On your turn pick up all the seeds in one of your pits and drop them one at a time into the pits that follow, round your store and on to the other side (never into their store).
  • If your last seed lands in your store, go again.
  • If your last seed lands in an empty pit of yours and the pit across has seeds, you capture both into your store.
  • The game ends when one side is empty. The other player keeps the seeds left on their side, and the bigger store wins.
