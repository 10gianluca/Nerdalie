How to play
  • On your turn do one of these: take one goods card from the market, take all the camels, swap cards with the market, or sell goods.
  • Your hand holds at most 7 cards. Camels go in your herd and don't count.
  • Swap two or more: give goods from your hand and/or camels, take the same number of goods from the market. You can't take camels, or take a kind you are giving.
  • Sell cards of one kind. Each card earns a token, highest first. Diamonds, gold and silver must go two or more at a time. Selling 3, 4 or 5+ also earns a face-down bonus token.
  • The round ends when three kinds of tokens run out, or the market can't be refilled. The bigger herd gets 5 points.
  • Win a round for a seal of excellence. Two seals win the match.
