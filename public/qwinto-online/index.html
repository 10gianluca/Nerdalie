Qwinto

The dice game, online with a friend

or join a friend's game
How to play
  • Players take turns rolling. On your turn, pick 1–3 dice and roll. You may reroll once, but it has to be the same dice.
  • Both players may then write the sum into one field of a row that matches a rolled die's color.
  • Numbers in a row must go up from left to right. The same number can't appear twice in a column.
  • If the roller doesn't write the sum down, they take a failed attempt (−5). The other player can skip for free.
  • The game ends when someone completes two rows or takes their 4th failed attempt.
  • Scoring: a full row scores its rightmost number, and any other row scores 1 point per number. When a column of 3 is full, you also score the number in its pentagon.
