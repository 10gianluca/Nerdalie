How to play
  • Each colour is an expedition. On your turn, play or discard one card, then draw one card.
  • Cards in an expedition must go up in number. The 🤝 wager cards double the result, but they have to be played before any number in that colour.
  • A card you don't want goes on that colour's discard pile. You may draw from the draw pile or the top of any discard pile, except the one you just discarded to.
  • Each expedition you start scores (total − 20) × (1 + wagers), plus 20 bonus for 8 or more cards. Start one you can't finish and it costs you.
  • The round ends the moment the draw pile runs out. A match is 3 rounds.
