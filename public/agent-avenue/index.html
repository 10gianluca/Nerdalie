Agent Avenue

The spy card game, online with a friend

or join a friend's game
How to play
  • Two spies chase each other around a circular track. Catch the other meeple by reaching or passing their space, and you win.
  • Each turn, the active player plays two cards with different names: one face-up, one face-down. The other player picks one and gets it. You keep the other.
  • Each card shows three numbers. How far you move depends on how many of that agent you have in play: the 1st, 2nd or 3rd+ number. Negative numbers move you backwards.
  • 3 Codebreakers in play wins the game. 3 Daredevils in play loses it.
  • Before playing, you can discard a card and redraw, up to 4 times a game.
  • If the deck runs out, whoever is closer to catching the other wins.
