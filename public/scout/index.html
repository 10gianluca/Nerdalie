Scout

The circus card game, online with a friend

or join a friend's game
How to play
  • Every card has two numbers, one at each end. At the start of a round you may turn your whole hand over to use the other numbers. After that you never rearrange your hand.
  • On your turn you Show: play neighbouring cards from your hand that make a set (the same number) or a run (numbers in a row, going up or down). It has to beat the set on the table: more cards, or a set instead of a run, or higher numbers. You win the cards you beat.
  • Or you Scout: spend a chip to take the left or right card of the set on the table and put it anywhere in your hand, either way up. Then you keep going until you Show or run out of chips. You have 3 chips each round.
  • A round ends when someone goes out, or when someone can't Show and has no chips.
  • Score: cards you won + unspent chips − cards left in your hand. The player nobody could beat keeps their hand for free. Two rounds, and the higher total wins.
