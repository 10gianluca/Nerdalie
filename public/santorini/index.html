Santorini

Build towers on a whitewashed island and reach the top first

or join a friend's game
or play on your own against the computer
How to play
  • Each player has two builders. You take turns putting them on the island, then each turn is a move and a build.
  • Move one builder to a neighbouring square (diagonals too). You can climb one level up at most, and drop down as far as you like. You can't step onto a builder or a dome.
  • Build next to the builder you just moved: add one block (level 1, then 2, then 3). On a level 3 tower you build a dome, which closes the square for good.
  • Win by moving a builder up onto level 3. You also win if the other player can't take a full turn.
  • Domes are how you stop a tower the other player is about to climb.
