How to play
  • Pieces sit on the dark squares. A piece moves one square diagonally forward. When it reaches the far row it becomes a king and may move diagonally in any direction.
  • To capture, jump diagonally over an enemy piece next to you onto the empty square just beyond. Keep jumping if you can.
  • Jumping is compulsory. If you can capture, you must. If there is a choice you pick which jump.
  • A man that reaches the far row is crowned and its move ends there.
  • You win when the other player has no pieces left or cant move. Forty moves each with no capture and no man moving is a draw.
