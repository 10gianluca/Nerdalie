How to play
  • There are 16 pieces, each tall or short, dark or light, round or square, hollow or solid.
  • On your turn you place the piece you were given on any empty square, then choose a piece for your opponent to place next.
  • Make a line of four (across, down or diagonal) that share at least one feature and you win, for example four dark pieces, or four hollow ones.
  • So never hand over a piece that finishes a line. If the board fills up with no line, it is a draw.
