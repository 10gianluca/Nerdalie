How to play
  • You and your friend play together against the Sanderson sisters. There's no losing to each other — you both win, or both lose.
  • 5 Ingredient piles sit in the cauldron. Each turn: optionally use a one-time Trick, then play a card from your hand onto a pile it matches by color or by type (or say you can't, which draws a Spell).
  • If all 5 piles ever show the same color, Sarah is stunned. All same type stuns Mary — unless the 5 piles are also all different colors, which stuns Winifred instead. A stunned witch's Spells miss until the next stun.
  • 3 stuns and the sun rises — you win! Drawing a Spell that forces you to draw more cards than are left in the deck ends the game as a loss.
  • Special cards: the Spell Book symbol draws a Spell immediately; Binx reveals a hand (yours or your friend's, your choice) until the next stun.

The deck size, colors, ingredient types, special symbols, Tricks, and win/lose conditions follow the official rulebook. The exact text of all 13 Spell cards and the cauldron's starting pairs aren't spelled out there, so those are this app's own reasonable design.
